## 2024-05-18 - Caching fully-grown canvas geometry in rAF loops
**Learning:** Canvas rendering performance in this codebase (like `birthday.js`) is heavily bottlenecked by repetitive calculation of static geometry (e.g., quadratic curve interpolation in `quad()`, and `lerp()` for width) during continuous `requestAnimationFrame` loops. Even though the mathematical functions seem small, invoking them hundreds of times per frame across numerous objects (branches, in this case) causes severe CPU overhead.
**Action:** When animating items that reach a final static state but still need to be redrawn every frame (e.g., fully-grown branches), calculate the final geometry/style once and cache the drawing primitives (segments, widths). Iterate over the cache for subsequent frames rather than re-running the mathematical interpolation.
